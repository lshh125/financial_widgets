Assumptions
  • Medical expense means allowed charges. You pay all of it up to the deductible, then the coinsurance share, up to the out-of-pocket maximum. Copays are not modeled.
  • A plan counts as an HSA-eligible HDHP when its deductible is at least $1,700 (self-only) or $3,400 (family). Other plans use a health FSA.
  • Premiums, FSA, and HSA contributions go through payroll pre-tax, so each dollar saves the tax rate you pick.
  • FSA election is the chosen % of that plan's out-of-pocket cost at each expense level, capped at $3,400. Any election above what you spend is forfeited (no carryover or grace period).
  • HSA money is yours even if unspent, so the full contribution counts. The employer HSA contribution lowers the HDHP's cost and counts toward the $4,400 / $8,750 limit (+$1,000 at 55+).
